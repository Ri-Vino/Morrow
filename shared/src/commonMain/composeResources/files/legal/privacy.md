# Privacy policy

Morrow · Last updated 18 October 2026

Morrow is an alarm clock made by Rivino. This policy explains what Morrow does with information about you. In short: Morrow has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and what you put into it stays on your phone.

## Who is responsible

Morrow is made and published by Rivino (https://github.com/Ri-Vino). "We" in this policy means Rivino.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you set up in Morrow is kept on your phone, in the app's own storage:

- your alarms, with their labels, repeat days, sounds and wake-up games
- your timers and their notes
- the cities you save on the World tab, and your home city
- your settings, such as weather units, Pip's voice and the volume ramp
- the code you choose for a Scan game
- sounds you bring in from your phone, which are copied into the app
- recent weather forecasts and online city searches, so the app works offline; searches are kept for up to 30 days
- your rough location from the last weather update, rounded to about 1 km, and the name of the city it's in

None of this is sent to us. We have no server, and no way to see it.

On Android, Morrow opts out of Android's backup, so this data isn't copied to your Google account. On iPhone, it's part of your phone's own backup to iCloud or your computer, which you and Apple control, not us.

Uninstalling Morrow deletes all of it.

## What leaves your phone: weather

Weather is the only part of Morrow that uses the internet. It's on when you first install Morrow, and you can turn it off in Settings.

While weather is on, Morrow asks Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com), a weather service, for forecasts. It sends:

- for "Where I am" weather: your rough location, rounded to 2 decimal places (about 1 km)
- for your home city and the cities on your World tab: each city's position
- when you search for a city that isn't in Morrow's built-in list: what you type, once it's at least 2 characters

These requests are encrypted (HTTPS) and carry no account, identifier or cookies, only the app's name and version. Like any website, Open-Meteo sees your internet (IP) address when it answers. Open-Meteo's own terms and privacy policy are on its website.

Morrow asks for weather only while the app is on screen, at most every 30 minutes for each place. With weather off, Morrow sends nothing at all, and city search uses only the built-in list.

## Location

Location is optional, and is used only for "Where I am" weather. Morrow asks for your approximate location, never your precise one. It reads it only while Morrow is on screen, including while an alarm is ringing so your Morning screen has local weather, and never in the background.

If you don't allow location, Morrow uses your home city, or the city for your phone's time zone, instead.

## Camera

The camera is used only during a Scan game, to read the barcode or QR code you chose. Codes are read on the phone. No pictures are saved or sent anywhere.

## Motion, voice and notifications

- The Shake game reads the phone's motion sensor while the game is on screen.
- Pip's voice uses your phone's own text-to-speech voices. Morrow doesn't send what Pip says anywhere.
- Notifications, exact alarms, and showing over other apps are used only to ring your alarms and timers on time.

## What Morrow doesn't do

- no accounts or sign-in
- no ads
- no analytics, crash reporting or tracking
- no selling or sharing of your information
- no background location

Morrow doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

## Your choices

- Turn weather off in Settings, and Morrow stops using the internet.
- Take back location or camera access at any time in your phone's settings.
- Delete alarms, timers and cities in the app, or uninstall Morrow to delete everything.

Because we hold no information about you, there's nothing for us to hand over or delete. It's all on your phone, under your control.

## Changes to this policy

If Morrow starts doing anything new with your information, this policy will change first, and so will the date at the top. The current policy is always in the app, under Settings, Legal & about, and in Morrow's source code on GitHub.
