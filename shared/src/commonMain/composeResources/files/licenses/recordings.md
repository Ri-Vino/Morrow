# Recorded alarm sounds

Morrow's Classical alarm sounds are the first 24 seconds of these recordings, cut by `tools/clip_recordings.py`.
Each recording, not just the music, was released into the public domain, as its Wikimedia Commons page says.
Musopen asks for credit where its recordings are used, which Settings gives.
